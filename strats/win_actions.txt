FINITE DUNGEONS - WINNING ACTIONS, ROUND BY ROUND
=================================================

For the current balance (Elixir costs 2, Vitality takes 10%, bronze card at 2).

Follow every line exactly: the game has no randomness, so these exact moves
always give the same result. One extra or missing purchase can change it.
Explanations are in HOW_TO_WIN.md.

Each line: the level, what you should have when that level's menu opens
(HP and money), what to buy, and the keys to press. The keys are the same on
PC and calculator: 1 opens the Shop, type each item number, 0 goes back,
0 starts the fight. Press Enter (PC) or EXE (calculator) after each number.

Shop numbers (the same in all three runs):
  1 Sword   2 Shield   3 Boots   4 Chicken   5 Book of Attack   6 Book of Defence
  7 Book of Speed   8 Book of Max HP   9 Book of Healing   0 back to the level menu
